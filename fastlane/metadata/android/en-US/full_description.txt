UntisPlus is a free and open source Android client for the Untis timetable
system. It is a hard fork of BetterUntis with its own application ID, so it
can be installed side by side with the original app.

Features:
- Modern Material 3 interface built with Jetpack Compose
- Fast timetable loading with smart caching
- Multiple separate accounts and profiles
- RoomFinder to find free rooms
- Info Center for events, contact hours, messages and your own absences
- Parent/guardian views with tabs for all children and each child
- Teacher class tools such as absence checking and lesson topic editing
- Break notifications and automatic muting during lessons
- Home screen widgets

UntisPlus can also open read-only details for office hour registrations. It
never books or changes registrations.

Untis is proprietary timetable software provided by schools. To use
UntisPlus, your school must use Untis and give you an account unless
anonymous access is available.

UntisPlus contains no Google Play Services, Firebase, ads, tracking or
in-app purchases. QR scanning uses an embedded open source scanner.

The official Untis service is not free software; see the Anti-Features note.
