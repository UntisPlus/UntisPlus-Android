UntisPlus is an open-source mobile client for the Untis timetable system
which allows you to view your timetable on your smartphone. It is a hard
fork of BetterUntis with its own application ID, so it can be installed
side by side with the original app.

The app allows customization of almost any aspect of your timetable,
including multiple themes and dark mode.

Easily add multiple profiles with separate settings and timetables.

Protect your privacy: UntisPlus contains no Google Play Services, Firebase,
ads, tracking or in-app purchases. QR scanning uses an embedded scanner.

Use notifications to show information about the next lesson.

Other features include a room finder to search for free rooms in your
school, an info center with events, contact hours, messages and absences,
teacher class tools, parent/guardian views and home screen widgets.

Enjoy a fast and smooth experience with UntisPlus.

Untis is proprietary timetable software. More information about Untis can be
found at https://untis.at.
To use Untis, your school must provide it.
Your school must also give you an account which you can use to access
your current timetable if no anonymous access is available.

Why use this app when there is an official one?
The official app is proprietary and includes tracking software and
in-app purchases. UntisPlus is free and open source, feature-rich and
respects your privacy.

WebUntis is a proprietary network.
